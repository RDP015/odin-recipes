Hamburguer

Description

The classic hamburguer, bread, meat an cheese

Ingredients

  • Two slice of bread
  • 200gr of meat 90/10
  • 5ml of oil
  • 1 slice of cheese

Steps

  1. Cook the meat at medium therm
  2. Put the cheese over the meat for 30 seconds
  3. Join the meat between the slices of bread and enjoy
