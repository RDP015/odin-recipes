Toqueres

Description

Toquere is a tierra caliente dish that is made of corn masa

The result is like a tortilla but more thick, you can eat it with milk, salsa or acid cream

Ingredients

  • 12 corns
  • 250gr of piloncillo
  • 50gr of wheat flour
  • 60ml of whater

Steps

  1. Prepare the candy
  2. Process the corn
  3. Mix with the masa
  4. Cook in a oven
